Hermes avatar states
Every state loops. After a task Hermes goes back to idle, and falls asleep (screen dims) after a long quiet spell.

Waiting for you
Listening
Hearing your voice
Thinking
Working out a reply
Working
Running a tool or skill
Searching
Looking something up
Speaking
Reading the reply aloud
Done
Task finished
Oops
Something went wrong
Sleeping
Quiet for a while, screen dims
Happy
Good news or a compliment
Alarm
Reminder or timer going off
Singing
